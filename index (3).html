faq

questions you might have

Is Habit Tracker free?

Yes. It's completely free to use in your browser.

Do I need to make an account?

Nope. Open the app, tap Add habit and you're in.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. It's a responsive web app, so it works on phones, tablets and computers. Add it to your home screen for one-tap access.

What is the monthly report?

It's a look back at your month so you can see how consistent you were with each habit and adjust where needed.

Is there a dark mode?

Yes. Use the sun/moon button at the top of the app to switch between dark and light themes.

Can I turn the sound off?

Yes. Use the speaker button in the app to mute or unmute sounds.
